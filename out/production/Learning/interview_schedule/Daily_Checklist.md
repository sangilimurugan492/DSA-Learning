## 📖 How to Use This Checklist

1. **Click a week below** to open that week's daily checklist
2. **Each day has checkboxes** — tick them as you complete each item
3. **Weekdays (Mon–Fri):** ~2.5–3 hrs (45 min Android + 45 min Flutter + 30 min DSA + 10 min review)
4. **Weekends (Sat–Sun):** ~4–5 hrs (deep dive + 4–6 DSA + **1 EXTRA bonus topic**)
5. **🔄 Cross-Platform Insight:** Compare Android vs Flutter concepts daily
6. **Spaced Repetition:** Revisit yesterday's topic for 5 min
7. **Self-Check:** Answer the end-of-day questions before moving on
8. **Don't skip DSA** — even on theory-heavy days, solve at least 1 problem

## 🧮 DSA Problem Format

Each DSA problem in the weekly checklists follows this format:

```
- [ ] **[Main Topic › Sub Topic | Difficulty]** Problem Name (est. time) — [SolutionFile.kt](link)
```

**Example:**
```
- [ ] **[Array › Two Pointer | 🟡 Medium]** Three Sum (30 min) — [ThreeSum.kt](../DSA/array/two_pointer/three_sum/ThreeSum.kt)
```

### Difficulty Levels:
- 🟢 **Easy** — Basic pattern recognition, <15 min
- 🟡 **Medium** — Multi-step reasoning, <25 min
- 🔴 **Hard** — Complex algorithms, <40 min
