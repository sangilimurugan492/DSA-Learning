# Behavioral Interview Questions

Behavioral interview questions with STAR-format answers, organized by technology.

## STAR Format
- **S**ituation — Context and background
- **T**ask — What you needed to accomplish
- **A**ction — What you specifically did
- **R**esult — The outcome and impact

## Question Categories

Each file covers these common behavioral question types:
- **Migration / Technical transition** — Moving from one tech/approach to another
- **Performance optimization** — Identifying and fixing bottlenecks
- **Architecture decision** — Choosing between approaches
- **Production incident** — Debugging under pressure
- **Mentoring** — Helping team members grow
- **Stakeholder management** — Communicating with non-technical people
- **Disagreement resolution** — Handling technical conflicts
- **Tech debt vs delivery** — Balancing quality with speed
- **Failure & learning** — Owning mistakes and growing
- **Driving adoption** — Introducing new practices/tools

## How to Use
1. Read the question
2. Think about your own experience
3. Compare with the provided STAR answer
4. Adapt the structure to your own stories
5. Practice speaking the answer aloud (2-3 minutes per answer)

## Tips
- Be specific — use real numbers, timelines, and technologies
- Focus on YOUR actions — use "I" not "we"
- Show impact — quantify results where possible
- Show growth — include what you learned
- Have 5-7 stories ready that can adapt to different questions
- Practice out loud — the delivery matters as much as the content
- Don't memorize word-for-word — know the key points and speak naturally
