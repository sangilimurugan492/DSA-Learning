## 📝 Daily Protocol (45–60 min)

1. **Read** the day's questions from the linked question bank (10 min)
2. **Self-answer aloud** — explain each answer as if in an interview (25–30 min)
3. **Write a 3-line summary** of what you learned (5 min)
4. **Mark the day** ✅ in [Progress_Tracker.md](Progress_Tracker.md) (1 min)
5. **Sunday extra:** 15-min self-quiz — recall this week's 5 topics from memory
6. **Sunday extra:** 10-min behavioral practice — answer 1–2 STAR questions aloud from [behavioral_questions/](../behavioral_questions/) (build your story bank week by week)
