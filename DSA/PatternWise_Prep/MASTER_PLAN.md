## 📝 The Hint-Reveal Protocol (IMPORTANT)

For every problem, follow this exact order:

1. **Read the problem statement.** Do NOT look at hints.
2. **Try to solve from scratch** (10–15 min for Easy, 20–25 min for Medium, 30–35 min for Hard).
3. Stuck? → Reveal **Hint 1** (pattern name only). Try again for 10 min.
4. Still stuck? → Reveal **Hint 2** (key insight). Try again for 10 min.
5. Still stuck? → Reveal **Hint 3** (step-by-step approach). Now write the code YOURSELF.
6. After solving → ✅ Compare with your existing solution in `DSA/` folder.
7. Log your result in [Progress_Tracker.md](Progress_Tracker.md).

**Status marks:**
- ⬜ Not attempted
- 🧠 Solved with no hints
- 💡 Solved after hints
- 📖 Had to read solution
- 🏆 Solved from scratch 3 times = Mastered

## 🧪 Sunday Mock Test Format

- **Duration:** 2 hours, timed
- **Questions:** 4–5 problems (mix: 1 Easy, 2–3 Medium, 1 Hard)
- **Mix:** 60% current week's patterns + 40% older patterns (spaced repetition)
- **Rules:** No hints for first 30 min. Hints allowed after, but log it.
