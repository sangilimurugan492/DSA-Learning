## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Consistency > Intensity** — 2 hours daily beats 10 hours on weekend
2. **Active recall** — After reading, close the file and explain out loud
3. **Code by hand** — Practice writing code on paper/whiteboard
4. **Spaced repetition** — Revisit topics from previous weeks
5. **Mock interviews** — Practice with a friend or record yourself
6. **Focus on understanding** — Don't memorize, understand the "why"
7. **Track mistakes** — Keep a log of problems you got wrong
8. **Time yourself** — Aim for 20 min per easy, 30 min per medium, 45 min per hard
